A VAE trained on legitimate traffic reconstructs the transaction. High reconstruction error means unusual.

What does the score mean?

The score is the negative log-likelihood of the transaction under the VAE, summed over the observed fields. Each field's share of it is shown on the receipt, so you can see which fields the model could not explain.
